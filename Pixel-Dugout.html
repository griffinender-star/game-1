WELCOME TO THE SHOW.

One thumb. A little timing. A whole lot of baseball.

01 / AT BAT

Wait. Watch. Swing.

Tap READY FOR PITCH. Watch the ball travel to your batter on the left, then tap SWING when the timing needle reaches the green zone. Perfect timing means harder contact. Follow the ball and baserunners on the full field after contact. Tap CONTINUE when you are ready, or REPLAY to see the play again. Let outside pitches pass with TAKE.

02 / ON THE MOUND

Find your corner.

Drag the crosshair near the batter to aim. Hold PITCH to charge the meter, then release in the green for your best pitch. Aim outside the dotted strike zone to chase a swing, but risk a ball.

03 / QUICK BASEBALL

Three innings. All heart.

Quick games play three innings, with extra innings for ties. Choose nine innings when starting a new season. Four balls earn a walk; three strikes make an out; three outs switch sides.

04 / YOUR CLUB

Build your future.

Create a manager and rebuild a weak Knights roster. Draft prospects, trade players using club credits, and develop your squad between seasons in the Front Office. Reorder your lineup before the first pitch, bring in a fresh pitcher, or pinch-hit from the bench. Try a steal with a runner on first and second empty. SIM HALF fast-forwards the current half inning.

05 / PHONE READY

A ballpark in your pocket.

Play in portrait or landscape. Open this single file in a browser that supports local HTML. Progress saves in this browser. All graphics and game code are included; no connection is needed.

06 / KEYBOARD TOO

Room for everyone.

Space readies a pitch or swings. Hold and release Space to pitch. Arrow keys adjust pitching aim. Escape pauses or closes a dialog. Sound is optional, and reduced-motion settings are respected.
