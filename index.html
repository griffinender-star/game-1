Pick up & play

Welcome to the show.

One thumb. A little timing. A whole lot of baseball.

01 / At bat

Wait. Watch. Swing.

Tap Start at-bat. Watch the ball travel to the plate, then tap Swing or the field just as it reaches your batter. Perfect timing means harder contact. Follow the ball and baserunners on the full field after contact. Tap CONTINUE when you are ready, or REPLAY to see the play again. Let outside pitches pass with Take pitch. Choose Relaxed for more forgiving timing or All-Star for a challenge. Flow play moves to the next play automatically; turn it off to review each result at your own pace.

02 / On the mound

Pick your location.

Tap or drag in the separate strike-zone panel to choose your target, then tap Pitch. The inner box is the strike zone. Numbered dots show where your actual pitches land. Pitcher control affects accuracy; aim outside the box to tempt a chase.

03 / Quick baseball

Three innings. All heart.

Quick games play three innings, with extra innings for ties. Choose nine innings when starting a new season. Four balls earn a walk; three strikes make an out; three outs switch sides.

04 / Your club

Build your future.

Create a manager and rebuild a weak Knights roster. Draft prospects, trade players using club credits, and develop your squad between seasons in the Front Office. Reorder your lineup before the first pitch, bring in a fresh pitcher, or pinch-hit from the bench. Try a steal with a runner on first and second empty. SIM HALF fast-forwards the current half inning.

05 / Phone ready

A ballpark in your pocket.

Play in portrait or landscape. Use your browser’s Add to Home Screen option for an app-like view. Progress saves in this browser. Offline play becomes available after loading once from a web server.

06 / Keyboard too

Room for everyone.

Space starts an at-bat, swings, or throws a pitch. Arrow keys adjust your target in the side strike zone. Escape pauses or closes a dialog. Sound is optional, and reduced-motion settings are respected.
